# Competitor Analysis & Keyword Gaps

## The competitive set, in three tiers

Not every site ranking for "internship" is a competitor. Three groups behave very differently, and only one of them is worth fighting.

### Tier 1 — Marketplaces. Do not attack head terms.

**Internshala**, **Unstop**, **LinkedIn**, **Naukri Campus**, **Indeed**, **Foundit**.

Internshala positions itself as India's No. 1 career platform with 120K+ paid internships, jobs and online courses, and describes itself as trusted by 400K+ companies. Unstop lists 795+ verified internships across work-from-home, remote and paid formats.

These are aggregators with enormous domain authority, hundreds of thousands of indexed listing pages, and mobile apps. Their weakness is the one your own program already identifies: students report a high volume of unpaid or low-paying listings and slow response times, which is why many are now looking for alternatives. That weakness is not exploitable on "internships in India". It is exploitable on the specific, qualified queries below.

### Tier 2 — The real competitors. Same offer, same audience.

**CodSoft**, **Oasis Infobyte**, **Prodigy InfoTech**, **Zidio Development**, **InternshipGate**, **Forage**, and the government-backed **AICTE / TULIP** and **EduSkills** portals.

This is the directly comparable set: free or near-free, task-based, remote, certificate-on-completion. CodSoft's model is virtually identical — a form-based application with no resume, no interview, open selection, and a certificate plus letter of recommendation after 3–4 weeks of task submissions.

**Where FreeInternships.in can win against this tier:**

| Differentiator | Status |
|---|---|
| QR-verified, publicly checkable certificate registry | **Owned, unique in tier, under-promoted** |
| Mentor-evaluated deliverables rather than auto-issued certificates | **Owned, stated well on the homepage** |
| Certificate carries project name and evaluation score | **Owned, barely mentioned** |
| Duration range 15 days to 6 months | **Owned; competitors mostly offer one fixed length** |
| 12+ domains including non-tech (HR, Finance, Logistics) | **Owned; CodSoft and Prodigy are tech-only** |
| Letter of recommendation | **Not offered — CodSoft offers this and students ask for it** |
| Government/AICTE affiliation | **Not held — AICTE/TULIP win on institutional trust** |

**Where it currently loses:** the ₹499 contradiction (content.md C2). Every Tier 2 competitor is unambiguously free. A student comparing options finds a site called freeinternships.in that mentions a fee on its Digital Marketing page, and closes the tab. This single inconsistency is worth more than any ranking factor on this list.

### Tier 3 — Content competitors. These are the ones outranking you today.

**theinternship.in**, **traffictail.com**, **thevistaacademy.com**, **internshipuncle.in**.

Small blogs with modest authority that occupy the informational queries your buyers actually search: "Free Internship with Certificate 2026: Government & Online Options" covering AICTE, TULIP, Forage and EduSkills; "Top 10 Platforms For Free Internships With Certificate 2026"; "10 Best Internships for Students 2026 | Verified Platforms".

These listicles rank for the exact queries your students type before they know any brand name — and **FreeInternships.in does not appear in them.**

**This is the most actionable competitive finding in the audit.** It is also the cheapest to act on: these are small sites that accept submissions and update their roundups. Getting listed in four or five of them is a week of outreach and it puts the brand in front of pre-qualified traffic. Do this before any technical work on link building.

## Keyword gaps

The site already targets head and mid-tail commercial terms well ("free internship with certificate", "work from home internship", "free internship Chennai", "internship for CSE students"). The gaps are in the informational and comparative layers that sit above and beside those terms.

### Gap 1 — Comparison and evaluation queries

Nothing on the site addresses the question every student asks before converting:

- "is free internship with certificate valid"
- "free internship vs paid internship"
- "are online internship certificates recognised by companies"
- "how to verify an internship certificate"
- "virtual internship vs online course"
- "CodSoft vs Internshala vs ..." and similar comparison terms

**This is where the certificate verification tool becomes a content asset rather than a page.** An article titled "How to Verify an Internship Certificate (and Why Most Can't Be Verified)" answers a real query, demonstrates the differentiator, and is linkable by recruiters. Nobody in Tier 2 can write it credibly.

### Gap 2 — Branch and degree segmentation

`free-internship-with-certificate-online-for-cse-students.php` exists and works. The same page does not exist for ECE, IT, MCA, BCA, MBA, Mechanical, Civil, B.Sc or BBA — all high-volume student segments explicitly welcomed in the FAQ.

### Gap 3 — City coverage beyond Chennai

Four Chennai landing pages exist. There are none for Pune, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Delhi, Mumbai, Coimbatore or Kolkata. The program is 100% remote, so there is no delivery constraint — only a content gap. Note that this has to be handled carefully: near-identical city pages are exactly the doorway-page pattern Google penalises. Each needs genuinely local substance (colleges served, local student testimonials, local placement outcomes) or it should not be built.

### Gap 4 — Roadmap hub

The 25 roadmap articles are the site's best content and they are orphaned in a blog sidebar. There is no `/roadmaps/` hub, no cross-linking between them, and no link from any program page.

**Fix:** Build a roadmap index page, link it from the main navigation, link each roadmap to its program page and back. This is a half-day of work that activates content that already exists.

### Gap 5 — Seasonal intent

"summer internship 2026", "winter internship", "internship for final year students", "internship before placement season". Zero coverage. Internshala owns these because it publishes for them every cycle.
